How I work

A simple, repeatable method that moves from ambiguity to a defensible position.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Business context first

What you do, what you owe your customers, what regulators expect and how much risk the business is actually willing to carry.

02 — ASSESS & QUANTIFY

Evidence, not opinions

Framework-based assessment and, where it matters, financial quantification of exposure so priorities are argued in numbers.

03 — PRIORITISE

Pragmatic remediation

A sequenced roadmap that reduces the most material risk first, sized to your team, budget and delivery reality.

04 — REPORT & DEFEND

Ready for scrutiny

Board-ready reporting and clear evidence trails so you can defend your position to auditors, investors and enterprise customers.
